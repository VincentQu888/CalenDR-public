Treatment begins with a proper schedule


The first step to consistent medical treatment is having a convenient, easy to follow schedule. Following the schedule CalenDR provides is an easy way to ensure you stay up to date on all vaccinations, cancer screenings and flu shots.

Save time

With CalenDR, you no longer need to worry about when or what to have scheduled check ups for.

Accurate, automated knowledge.


CalenDR automates the scheduling process for you to save time from your actual calendar. It follows government health guidelines from your country to ensure utmost accuracy and notifies you during the months of required appointments.

Consistency and reliability.


When it comes to your health, there should never be mistakes. For us, your health is our biggest priority. CalenDR remains consistent in our reminder dates and reliable in the dates we recommend for cancer screenings and immunizations.
